### 👋 A little about me

I'm Henrik, an identity and integration engineer at **[Fortytwo Technologies](https://fortytwo.io)**. I work where people, directories, applications, and occasionally questionable legacy decisions meet.

My professional sweet spot is **Identity & Access Management**: turning complex identity lifecycles into reliable, maintainable automation. I enjoy connecting the old world to the new — from **Microsoft Identity Manager** and **Active Directory** to **Microsoft Entra ID**, modern identity platforms, APIs, and cloud-native services.

Outside work, I run a homelab where *“this should be simple”* is usually the beginning of a very interesting weekend.

### 💡 Engineering philosophy

> **Automate the boring. Understand the complicated. Document the surprising.**

I believe good engineering makes complex systems easier to operate — not merely more impressive to look at. I value pragmatic architecture, clear ownership, secure defaults, and solutions that survive their original author going on vacation.
